The sheet must contain exactly three coordinated views of the same character:

- a full-body front view in a relaxed neutral pose;
- a three-quarter upper-body view that clearly shows face structure and hair volume;
- a close face view that clearly shows the eyes, nose, mouth, jaw, skin tone, hairline, and identity markers.

Use one quiet, neutral gray studio background. Keep lighting, scale, color, and rendering style consistent across all three panels. Preserve the approved character's body silhouette and adult or age-appropriate proportions. Do not add packaging, labels, captions, decorative borders, alternate outfits, new accessories, or invented angles. Do not turn a stylized character into a photorealistic person.

This is a production reference asset, not a redesign.
